**AI for Better Learning**

Hey! This is my final project for Web Design and Markup.

The main idea behind this website is simple: help students use AI as a **learning tool, not a replacement for their own thinking**.

The website takes you through four steps that show how to work with AI more effectively - from understanding what an assignment actually asks for, to writing a better prompt, checking the answer, and finally making the result your own.

The point is not to tell you to use AI more. It is to help you use it **better**.

**Website Structure**

The website is built around four main steps:

**(Home):** Introduces the idea behind the project and shows why the way you use AI can affect the quality of its answers.
**(Step 01: Understand Task):** Shows how to break an assignment down and figure out what you actually need before asking AI for help.
**(Step 02: Write Better Prompts):** Explains how to give AI enough context, a clear task, a useful format, and specific requirements.
**(Step 03: Evaluate AI Output):** Shows how to question AI answers, check their sources and logic, and avoid trusting confident-sounding information blindly.
**(Step 04: Make It Yours):** Focuses on turning an AI-generated draft into something you actually understand, adapt, and take responsibility for.
**(Step 05: Contact & Feedback):** A place to ask questions, report errors, suggest improvements, or simply share feedback about the project.

Each page builds on the previous one, creating one simple workflow:

**Understand → Write → Evaluate → Make It Yours**

**The idea behind it**

AI can give you an answer in seconds. That doesn't automatically make it a good answer.

**Use AI to learn better, not to think less.**
