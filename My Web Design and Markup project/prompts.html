STEP 02

Writing Better Prompts

The clearer the request, the less AI has to guess.

🚫 Weak prompt example

> "Write about ecology."

🖼️ <> 🎙️
↑

Too vague: AI doesn't know what you need, who it's for, or how the answer should look.

✅ Structured prompt example (and how to build one)

> "I am an environmental science student. Explain the main causes of ocean pollution in 5 short bullet points using statistics from recent years."

🖼️ <> 🎙️
↑

To the point: AI knows background and depth level, what your need is, how the answer should look, and specific expectations.

↓

Structure of a Strong Prompt

CONTEXT

"I am an environmental science student"

Your background or story → tells AI who you are and sets the depth level.

TASK

"Explain the main causes of..."

The core job → basically the same from what you read in your assignment guidelines.

FORMAT

"in 5 short bullet points"

The visual look → how the answer should be structured (bullets, essay, table, etc.).

REQUIREMENT

"using statistics from..."

Specific expectations → raw ideas or constraints you want AI to consider.

You asked. AI answered. Can you trust the answer?
