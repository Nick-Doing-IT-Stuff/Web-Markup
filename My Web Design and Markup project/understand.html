STEP 01

Understand the Task

Before asking AI for help, use your brain to break down what you actually need to do.

Let's break it down in 5 easy steps:

  1. 1. What do I need to deliver?
  2. 2. What is the topic of my assignment?
  3. 3. What are the key requirements?
  4. 4. Who is it for?
  5. 5. What can AI actually help me with?

💡 Pro tip: This methodology works for any project: essays, coding tasks, research, or math problems.

Real Assignment Example:

Write a 2-page analytical essay on the impact of automations on the labour force. The text must include at least 3 arguments with real life examples and be targeted as economics students.
