STEP 01 / UNDERSTAND TASK

Before You Ask AI, Know What You Need

AI cannot help you properly if you don't understand the task yourself first.

Break Down Your Assignment

Before opening any AI tool, spend 2 minutes answering four core questions:

  1. Goal: What is the final deliverable? (e.g., essay, code review, summary)
  2. Scope: What specific topics should be covered or excluded?
  3. Format: How should the output be structured? (e.g., bullet points, academic paragraphs)
  4. Constraints: What are the word limits, required citations, or guidelines?

Compare the Approach

🚫 Bad Approach
> Write my essay about renewable energy

Paste the raw prompt without context, relying entirely on AI to guess requirements.

✅ Good Approach
> Help me outline an essay analyzing solar vs wind energy efficiency for high-school students. Focus on cost and environmental impact.

Define clear constraints, audience, and goal before generating content.
